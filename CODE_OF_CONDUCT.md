# Community conduct

DeskUnlock welcomes constructive participation from people with different experience levels and backgrounds.

Be respectful, discuss technical ideas rather than attacking people, assume good faith where reasonable, and keep reviews focused on the code, documentation, security, and user impact.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publication of another person's private credentials or device identifiers are not acceptable.

Security disagreements should be handled carefully and with evidence. Vulnerability reports should follow `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.
